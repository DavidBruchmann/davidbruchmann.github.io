More about the shown steps

🔴 Concept & Architecture

A website concept might depend on Corporate Identity and Design (CI / CD), and includes in most cases design and primarily functional requirements. The concept respects at best all constraints and advices, and might already give an idea about a technical solution. Many different types of websites have certainly also different conceptual and technical requirements.

🟢 Core & Integration

Building the foundational system by installing development environment and framework(s). Integrating Technical requirements, often a combination of standard and individual elements. Integration of media and content is sometimes done by the customer himself, then for development replaceable dummy-content is taken.

🔵 Systems & DevOps

Replicable environments like Docker and DDEV enable developers to work under same technical conditions, standartise automated testing and further workflows. In larger teams the according staff might also be responsible for all technical sever-related topics which can stretch much farther than simple server setups. Controlled package-management determines the technical functionality of all components.

🟠 Continuous Deployment

Safe and predictable releases. By building Continuous Integration workflows, websites are automatically tested right before deployment and have zero downtime or user disruption.
